Escrow

Agents with a payout may lock funds in a non-custodial smart contract (EscrowVault) rather than a BuildOS-controlled wallet. BuildOS cannot access, freeze, or redirect escrowed funds early — release is enforced by contract code once the unlock time you chose has passed. A 1% protocol fee is deducted on withdrawal. If a recipient does not claim funds within 90 days of unlock, the original depositor may reclaim them.
